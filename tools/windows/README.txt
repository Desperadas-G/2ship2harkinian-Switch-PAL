2 Ship 2 Harkinian - Switch PAL
https://github.com/Desperadas-G/2ship2harkinian-Switch-PAL

No ROM and no game data are included. Use a dump of your own cartridge.

[English]
1. Drop your Majora's Mask ROM (.z64, .n64, .v64 or a .zip) on "Generate mm.o2r.bat".
   You can also put the ROM in this folder and double-click the .bat.
2. Wait about a minute. switch\2s2h\mm.o2r is created.
3. Copy the "switch" folder to the root of your SD card.
4. Launch 2Ship from the Homebrew Menu, opened by holding R while starting a game.
Supported: PAL 1.1 / Rev A (Europe), NTSC-U 1.0 (USA), NTSC-U GameCube. Never share your mm.o2r.
